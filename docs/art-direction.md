## Research before generation

- [Lusion](https://lusion.co/): reviewed its actual homepage in the browser, including the large interactive sculptural opening and immediate statement of purpose. Principle used: make art the primary spatial experience while keeping the offer legible. No reference assets copied.
- [Universal Everything](https://www.universaleverything.com/): reviewed the practice’s description of living motion, responsive sound, prototypes and immersive ecosystems. Principle used: a coherent visual language can span several disciplines.
- [Nielsen Norman Group: Scrolling and Attention](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/scrolling-and-attention/): its 2018 eye-tracking study supports front-loading purpose and key actions, and giving visitors reasons to continue. It does not establish a retention uplift for this site.

## Design decisions

Keep the original monochrome character and tagline. Replace the long mandatory mythological prologue with a human story: purpose → curiosity → disciplines → human connection → collaboration → invitation. Original art establishes materiality, while a perspective-projected three-dimensional particle sculpture recalls the original ink system. Use natural scrolling, readable HTML text, keyboard-operable disciplines, reduced-motion support, and a user-controlled motion toggle. The project brief prepares a mailto draft for the visitor to send; nothing is submitted to a server.

Artwork is conceptual, not a claim of client work. No client logos, outcomes, testimonials or invented project case studies were added. Retention has not been measured. Evaluate story reach, discipline interactions and inquiry starts/completions with consent-appropriate analytics before claiming improvement.

### Becoming — hero and discipline study

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: immersive website hero art for doingthings, an independent product and creative studio, no UI included.
Primary request: A monumental, physically believable abstract sculpture that feels like an idea becoming alive. A single irregular looping form, between a hand-formed ribbon of liquid platinum, a weathered meteorite and a seed breaking open. It has an off-center opening, asymmetric folded ridges, smooth mirror-silver highlights against porous dark graphite, beautifully imperfect tactile details. Delicate thin silver filaments peel away from its edges like threads of thought. Not a perfect torus. Not a heart icon. No neon or holographic colors.
Scene/backdrop: limitless almost-black photographic studio, pure near-black #080808 on left blending into deep charcoal around sculpture. Faint volumetric silver light from above right, very subtle atmospheric dust, no floor or horizon.
Composition/framing: wide landscape 1536x1024, sculpture occupies right 65% of frame, centered at 66% x and 48% y, extends vertically from 10% to 88%; left 30% should remain dark clear negative space for oversized white typography layered separately. Strong monumental scale, sharp sculptural silhouette, dramatic chiaroscuro.
Style/medium: high-end contemporary gallery sculpture photographed on medium format, brutal yet tender, art direction of a conceptual fashion editorial, physically based 3D materials with genuine photographic grain.
Color palette: strictly monochrome, black, charcoal, silver and soft ivory white. Crisp specular edges, nuanced midtones, restrained bloom.
Constraints: original artwork, no text, no letters, no logo, no UI, no watermark, no stock corporate motifs, no random geometric spheres.

### Together — human connection

Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Asset type: cinematic full-width story section image for doingthings, a human-centered independent product studio.
Primary request: Fine-art black-and-white photograph of two real human hands almost meeting, connected by a single thin gleaming silver filament. The hand entering from the lower left is a dark-skinned adult hand with palm turned delicately upward; the hand entering from upper right is also an adult hand, reaching naturally toward it. Natural gestures, tenderness and making, tangible human contact without a literal handshake. Only the forearms and hands visible. Realistic anatomy exactly five fingers per hand, no jewelry. The narrow silver thread rests between the fingertips as if an idea is passing between people.
Scene/backdrop: vast black photographic studio, a whisper of smoky charcoal atmosphere. Hands occupy outer left and outer right thirds. Center is mostly dark open negative space. Slanting soft light reveals natural pores and subtle skin details with silver highlights.
Style/medium: museum-quality editorial photography, analog black-and-white 35mm film, tactile deep blacks, restrained grain, soft cinematic falloff, honest human texture.
Composition: landscape 1536x1024, hands around the upper-middle of frame, wrists cropped naturally by the left and right edges. Leave central 50% and lower center dark enough for separately laid large white website typography. Intentional quiet drama.
Color: entirely monochrome, black, graphite, silver, soft white.
Constraints: no letters, no text, no UI, no logo, no watermark, no neon, no glow orb, no illustration, no religious imagery.
